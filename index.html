Running your studio on Trello?

Through my company, KTS (KayTSolutions), I set up time tracking for small and indie teams that already plan in Trello. Nobody has to start a timer or fill in a timesheet: the board you already use becomes the data.

Denda Games has used it since early 2026, and I still make improvements when they ask.

What you get

  • Time logged automatically when cards move between lists
  • A daily email with who worked on what, and where the gaps are
  • Estimates compared with real hours, so planning gets better
  • Holidays, leave and working hours taken into account

About me

I’m Katrīna, KayTee for short. I study Creative Media and Game Technologies at Saxion and I like both sides of making games: designing what the player sees, and organising how the team gets there.

I grew up on stage. Six years of theatre, a role in the film Namejs’ Ring, commercials and a Dragons’ Den pitch taught me to explain an idea to any room, whether that’s a design decision for an artist or the numbers for management.

In my internship at Denda Games, I turned a vague brief into a working system for 18 people. Afterwards I started my own company to keep improving it, and Denda is still my client.
