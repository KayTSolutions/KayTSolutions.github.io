Energy use, made readable

Most people only see their energy use once a year, as one big number on a bill. This app breaks it down so people can see when and where their power goes, and do something about it.

Type
School project
My role
UX and hi-fi UI design
Tools
Figma

The idea

The challenge was making data feel simple. Every screen answers one question a household actually asks, with one main chart and a short sentence that says what it means.

  • When is power cheapest today? A 24-hour clock shows use per hour, with the best and worst times written out underneath.
  • Am I doing better than last month? Two rings compare this month with the user’s own goal, in euros, not kWh.
  • What is on right now? Each room lists its devices with a switch and their current use.
  • Which months were high? Monthly bars per room, with months above the average in red.

Colour has one meaning everywhere: green is good, red needs attention, and yellow highlights buttons and active choices.
