Making giving feel like play

Donation apps usually look like web shops: a list of projects and a pay button. I redesigned the Dubai Charity Association app around game mechanics, so donors explore, unlock and see their impact grow.

Type
Concept redesign
My role
UX, game design and UI
Tools
Figma

How it works

  • A village instead of a list. The home screen is a pixel-art village. Each building is a project category, such as mosques, schools, wells and housing, so browsing feels like exploring.
  • Progress you can see. Every category has a progress bar with milestones that unlock as people donate.
  • Badges for real behaviour. Badges like First Brick, Faithful Giver and Call to Action reward a first donation, giving again, and inviting others, not just the amount.
  • A friendly leaderboard. Top donors per category stand on a podium, and you always see your own rank and badges in a highlighted row.
  • Grow&Build. A tab in the main navigation where your donations help the village grow, a reason to return after the first gift.

This is a concept, not an official app of the Dubai Charity Association. Photos of people are placeholders.
